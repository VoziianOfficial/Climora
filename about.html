Company story

Climora started from a simple problem: HVAC requests often feel scattered.

Homeowners may need help with AC performance, heating concerns, system replacement, airflow questions, or seasonal service timing. But the first step can quickly become confusing when every provider asks for different information.

Climora gives homeowners a more organized starting point. The platform helps structure the request by category, location, timing, and project context so homeowners can compare independent provider options more clearly.

Aggregator model

Climora helps with matching, not direct HVAC work.
