Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Climora collects, uses, and handles information submitted through this HVAC provider matching website.

Last updated: May 4, 2026
Overview Information We Collect How We Use Information Information Sharing Cookies Your Choices Security Contact

1. Overview

Climora is an independent matching platform that helps homeowners connect with independent local HVAC service providers. Climora does not perform HVAC installation, repair, replacement, maintenance, or heating and cooling services directly.

By using this website, submitting a form, calling a listed phone number, or interacting with this site, you acknowledge that information may be collected and used as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through forms, calls, emails, or website interactions. This may include:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • ZIP code, city, state, or general service location.
  • HVAC service category or project details.
  • Information about heating, cooling, airflow, thermostat, repair, or installation requests.
  • Communication preferences or form consent confirmations.

We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, device type, IP address, referring pages, pages viewed, and approximate usage data.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to support the HVAC matching request process, respond to inquiries, improve website functionality, and communicate with users about submitted requests.

  • To process homeowner matching requests.
  • To connect submitted requests with independent local providers or partner networks.
  • To respond to calls, emails, and form submissions.
  • To improve website layout, performance, and user experience.
  • To maintain records related to requests, consent, and communications.
  • To support legal, compliance, fraud prevention, and security purposes.

4. How Information May Be Shared

Submitted request information may be shared with independent local providers, service partner networks, call centers, marketing partners, analytics providers, hosting providers, or other vendors that help operate the website and request flow.

Independent providers are separate businesses. Climora does not control how an independent provider may communicate with you after receiving your request. Homeowners should review provider terms, privacy practices, licensing, insurance, and qualifications directly.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, analytics scripts, or similar technologies to support basic functionality, understand site usage, remember privacy preferences, and improve the request experience.

For more information, review our Cookie Policy.

6. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit a form or may contact Climora using the available email address for privacy-related questions. You may also adjust browser settings to limit cookies or delete stored browser data.

If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, contact us using the details listed below. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

7. Security

Climora uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for use by adults seeking homeowner service matching information. It is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

Climora may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
