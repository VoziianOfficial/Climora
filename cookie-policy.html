Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Climora may use cookies, local storage, pixels, analytics, and similar technologies on this HVAC provider matching website.

1. Overview

Climora uses cookies and similar technologies to support site functionality, remember privacy preferences, understand basic website usage, and improve the homeowner request experience.

Climora is an independent HVAC provider matching platform. It does not perform HVAC installation, repair, replacement, maintenance, or heating and cooling services directly.

2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies can include local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, tags, scripts, and device identifiers.

These technologies can help websites remember settings, measure usage, improve performance, support security, and make forms or navigation easier to use.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Climora may use the following categories of cookies or similar technologies:

  • Essential cookies: used for basic site functionality, form behavior, navigation, security, and privacy preference storage.
  • Preference cookies: used to remember choices such as cookie banner acceptance or decline status.
  • Analytics cookies: used to understand how visitors interact with pages, sections, forms, and navigation.
  • Marketing or measurement pixels: used to measure traffic sources, ad performance, or request flow effectiveness when enabled.

4. Local Storage

This website may use local storage to remember whether a visitor accepted or declined the privacy preference banner. Local storage is stored in your browser and may remain until you clear browser data or the website updates its storage behavior.

For example, Climora may store a simple preference value so the banner does not keep appearing after you make a choice.

5. Analytics and Usage Data

Analytics tools may collect information such as page views, approximate location, device type, browser type, referring pages, time on page, and interaction events.

This information helps Climora understand whether the site is usable, where visitors encounter friction, and how the request flow can be improved.

6. Third-Party Technologies

Some cookies, pixels, scripts, or analytics tools may be provided by third-party vendors or partner platforms. These third parties may process information according to their own privacy and cookie practices.

If a homeowner request is submitted, information may also be shared with independent providers, partner networks, or vendors that help operate the request flow, as described in the Privacy Policy.

7. Your Cookie Choices

You can accept or decline the website’s privacy preference banner when it appears. You may also manage cookies through your browser settings.

  • Delete cookies and site data from your browser.
  • Block cookies in browser settings.
  • Use browser privacy tools or extensions.
  • Clear local storage for this website.

Blocking some technologies may affect website functionality, form behavior, preference storage, or user experience.

8. Updates to This Cookie Policy

Climora may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
